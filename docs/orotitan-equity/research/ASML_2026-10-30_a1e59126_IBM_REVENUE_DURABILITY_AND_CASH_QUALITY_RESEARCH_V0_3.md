## 1. Result / decision-relevant uncertainty

**Accounting proof:** ASML has genuinely contracted time-based services, warranties and parts support in its Installed Base Management franchise, but **the available US GAAP annual and quarterly financial statements do not disclose the revenue and gross profit separately for (a) contracted recurring maintenance, (b) activity/usage-based maintenance and billable parts, and (c) episodic NXE/DUV upgrades**. Therefore **the fraction of 2026 IBM that is guaranteed/contractually recurring cannot be determined from source material**. A single durable percentage or a separately certified IBM Owner Earnings estimate would be manufactured.

**Usable result without breaking Research:** retain the actual full IBM revenue as **a mixed economic category**; separate contract-based recurring character by type; use **dated historical IBM revenue as stress anchors, not certified lower floors**; treat the extra 2026 growth as potentially upgrade-related but do **not** claim `all growth = upgrades`. For group cash flows, use annual 2023–2025 US GAAP historical FCF and Q3 2026 LTM working-capital sensitivity, without attributing the entire group CFO / FCFF to IBM.

## 4. What the accounting policy calls truly contract-based vs cyclical

[SEC 20-F FY2025 ASC 606 obligations, R42](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/937966/000162828026011378/R42.htm) gives:

| Revenue source | Recognition | Economic durability classification | Inference limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Service contracts** (full-service, limited labor, parts availability/usage) | Period of service, straight-line for typically fixed price; some variable on system performance | `CONTRACT_BASED_PERIOD_REVENUE` | Finite contract, renewal economics, duration/retention and revenue amount **not separately disclosed** |
| Extended / enhanced warranties | Separate obligation, straight-line over warranty coverage | `CONTRACT_BASED_PERIOD_REVENUE` | **Standard 12-month warranty has no separately allocated revenue** |
| **OnPulse** light-source maintenance | Monthly billed by pulses consumed | `INSTALLED_FLEET_USAGE_LINKED` | Repeat usage but cycle-sensitive; **not locked volume** |
| Billable parts/labor and spares | Usually delivery or sign-off, point-in-time | `REPEAT_PURCHASE_POTENTIAL` | Not a multi-year revenue guarantee; potentially recurring demand over fleet life |
| Licenses and related support | Across contracted period | `CONTRACT_TERM_REVENUE` | Contract duration and allocated euros unknown |
| **Field upgrades/options** | During installation, or delivery depending on performance obligation | `EPISODIC_PROJECT_OPTION` | CAPEX/uptime and customer economics dependent; management reported upgrade wave 2025/2026 |
| Relocations / field projects | As labor/work delivered | `PROJECT_NONCONTRACTUAL_RECURRING` | Project-by-project, potentially lumpy |

**2025 report attribution:** service-and-option growth 2025 driven by (i) installed-base growth, (ii) utilization for certain customers, (iii) more NXE upgrades — **company provides no percentage** for each cause. [Issuer 2025 report](https://investor.asml.com/annual-reports/2025/financials).

**2026 Q3 attribution:** IBM **EUR 3,288.8m**, up from 2026 Q2 **EUR 2,761.7m**; issuer cites strong productivity-upgrade demand, about **EUR 0.4bn IBM above internal guidance** primarily tied to upgrades. This is NOT an exactly disclosed upgrade revenue figure: never infer `UPGRADE_REVENUE_Q3=400m`, `RECURRING_IBM_Q3=3288.8-400`, or extrapolate 2026 Q3 upgrades as perpetual service. [Issuer Q3 release](https://www.investor.asml.com/news-releases/news-release-details/asml-reports-eu11.7-billion-total-net-sales-and-eu5.0-billion-net-income-q3-2026).

## 6. Cash-flow test — do not attribute group CFO or customer prepayments to IBM

2025 US GAAP ASC 606 revenue contracts [Note 2 / R13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/937966/000162828026011378/R13.htm):
- **Total** contract liabilities FY2025 **EUR 19,372.6m** (2024 **EUR 18,196.2m**); includes system and service obligations and *conditional non-cash rights*, not pure IBM cash.
- **EUR 11,516.6m of 2025 revenue** was recognized from prior contract liabilities; that is *not* EUR 11.5bn IBM recurring sales or cash paid in 2025.
- **EUR 14,013.3m** new or unconditional consideration linked to remaining obligations in 2025; not identical to cash customer deposits, as it includes **unconditional rights to consideration**.
- Issuer states customers' remaining performance obligations amount to **EUR 46.5bn at FY2025** and mainly comprise NXT/NXE/EXE systems; no extrapolation of RPO to IBM recurring gross profit.
- Current and noncurrent contract liability portions must be kept separate: the 2026 Q3 12-page report shows **only EUR 9,300.6m in the noncurrent contract-liability line**, whereas **2025 EUR 19,372.6m is all contract liabilities**. Do NOT subtract these or treat the difference as cash generation.
