## C003: adopting hybrid-bonding customers at FY2025

Two issuer-origin accounts disagree *about the same year-end*:

- FY2025 annual report, published March 2026: hybrid bonding adoption
  **18 customers** at year end 2025; more than 150 cumulative system
  orders; six integrated lines incorporating 30 hybrid bonders. Source:
  https://live.euronext.com/sites/default/files/esg_document_files/2026-03/062133_Annual_Report_2025_2026-03-16_15%3A55.pdf
  Relevant segments: Letter to Stakeholders; report board of management
  (printed pages 19 and 40).
- FY2025 results, released 2026-02-19: adoption increased to **18
  customers**, corroborating the annual report's published claim from the
  *same issuer*, not an independent customer count:
  https://www.globenewswire.com/fr/news-release/2026/02/19/3240803/0/en/be-semiconductor-industries-n-v-announces-q4-25-and-full-year-2025-results.html
- Q2-2026 earnings release, dated 2026-07-23: management retrospectively
  says adoption grew **from 15 customers at year-end 2025 to 21 at end
  Q2-2026**. Source:
  https://www.morningstar.com/news/globe-newswire/9766965/be-semiconductor-industries-nv-announces-q2-26-and-h1-26-results
  The source is an issuer press release syndicated by a news provider.
- During the Q2-2026 results call, CEO Richard Blickman described
  approximately **21** customers at Q2-2026 without resolving the
  historical FY2025 discrepancy. This is a call-transcript derivative:
  https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/BESI/transcripts/561557-q2-2026/

**Interpretation, not a resolution:** Two issuer-issued statements give
FY2025=18 while a later issuer-issued statement gives FY2025=15.
Definitions (ordered systems, trial/evaluation, commercial production,
unique group/customer and calendar scope) might differ; **there is no
direct source establishing which explanation, if any, is correct**.
The coexistence of 18 and 15 is a real temporal inconsistency rather
than proof of fraudulent reporting. Preserve both claims and the exact
publication dates until a primary reconciliation is found.

**Required next action:** locate the original Q2-2026 presentation and
analyst Q&A, compare customer definitions across dated investor materials,
search corrections/errata and any IR reconciliation, and independently
verify production-scale qualifications where public evidence permits.
An earnings-release count from the issuer is not independent corroboration.

**Disposition:** `C003=OPEN_CONTRADICTION`.
`RESEARCH_SUFFICIENT` not established; neither Research FINAL nor
handoff nor publication is authorized by this note.
